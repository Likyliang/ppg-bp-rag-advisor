# Risk Hotspots

- Medical boundary regression: diagnosis, prescribing or medication changes,
  emergency reassurance, and cuff-replacement claims.
- Credential leakage through config, logs, audit, metrics, process arguments, or Git.
- Catalog/config concurrent writes and stale artifact misclassification.
- Worker command injection, duplicate workers, orphan processes, and unsafe retries.
- Raw PDF copyright/access governance and accidental repository inclusion.
- Development authentication bypass reaching an internal or remotely accessible deployment.
